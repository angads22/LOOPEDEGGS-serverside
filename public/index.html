Our Story

STEMroots is a student-led nonprofit organization that runs STEM camps and workshops at schools across Ontario. As of April 2026, we have run over 50 free camps at local Montessori schools, public schools, and high schools.

Started by 3 motivated high school students, STEMroots has rapidly scaled into a trusted organization that brings quality interactive camps across a variety of fields, such as robotics and engineering.

🆓

Always Free

Every camp and workshop we run is completely free to schools and students.

🤝

Trusted Partner

Working with Montessori schools, public schools, and high schools across Ontario.

🚀

Rapid Growth

From 3 students to a province-wide network in just a few short years.

🔬

Real Experiments

Robotics, engineering, biology and beyond, always hands-on, never just slides.

Programs

Lessons That
Stick With Kids

All STEMroots lessons combine an informational presentation with a hands-on experiment. We have a variety of premade lessons, but we're also happy to design custom lessons to match your curriculum. We offer one-off lessons or multi-week programs.

🧪 Science
  • Chemical Reactions — Building lava lamps + invisible ink
  • Introduction to Physics — Egg drop
  • Biology & Plant Anatomy — Growing bean plants in different environments + coloured celery
🛠️ Engineering
  • Engineering to Solve Problems — Building boats
  • Engineering to Solve Problems — Building bridges
  • Engineering & Design — Egg drop
💻 Technology
  • Fundamentals of Robotics — Interactive game
  • Technology in Our World — Building circuits
  • Coding — Coding with Scratch
