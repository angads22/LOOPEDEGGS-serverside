The Problem

The Flaw Every
Farmer Knows

Small-scale egg incubators share one consistent, unavoidable flaw — and it's been silently reducing hatch rates for decades.

🔦

Candling Requires Opening

To check egg development, farmers must regularly open the incubator and candle each egg — shining a light through to inspect growth. Non-viable eggs must be removed immediately to prevent rot from spreading to the healthy batch.

But every time the incubator opens, the carefully controlled environment is disrupted.

🌡️

Temperature Spikes

Opening the chamber causes rapid temperature swings that stress developing embryos.

💧

Humidity Loss

Precisely maintained humidity — critical for development — drops the moment the lid opens.

❄️

Worse in Winter

Cold ambient air amplifies fluctuations, making winter incubation especially high-risk.

⏱️

Time Consuming

Manual candling, turning, and monitoring consume hours of a farmer's valuable time.

"There are no solutions to this problem on the market at a non factory-farm scale."

— Open Spaces Farms, Ontario Community Farm
The Solution

LifeLoop Automates
Everything

Every process that previously required opening the incubator is now handled entirely inside the chamber — automatically.

👁️

Computer Vision Candling

Built-in cameras use computer vision to analyze egg development on schedule — no human intervention, zero chamber exposure.

🤖

Automated Dud Removal

Non-viable eggs are automatically identified and removed from the batch, protecting healthy eggs from contamination.

📊

Egg Tracking

Every egg is individually tracked throughout the cycle, giving you full visibility into each embryo's progress.

🔄

Auto-Turning

Eggs are turned at optimal intervals automatically — a critical step for preventing embryo adhesion to the shell.

🔒

Lockdown Phase

The final days of incubation are managed automatically, with conditions optimized for hatching without any intervention.

📱

Companion App

Monitor your entire flock from anywhere. Real-time updates, alerts, and cycle insights — all from your phone.

" Farmers don't need to interact with LifeLoop at all until the incubation cycle completes. "
Process

Three Steps.
That's It.

01

Load & Start

Place your fertilized eggs in the LifeLoop chamber and start the cycle. That's the only thing you need to do.

02

Automated Monitoring

LifeLoop continuously manages temperature, humidity, turning, and candling. Duds are removed automatically. You receive real-time updates on your phone.

Our Journey

From Farm Problem
to Provincial Champion

The Beginning

A Real Problem at a Real Farm

We visited Open Spaces Farms — a real community farm in Ontario — to identify hard agricultural problems for the AGrobotics robotics competition. The candling problem stood out immediately: the farm told us flatly there were no solutions at a non-factory scale.

AGrobotics Entry

LifeLoop Is Born

We designed and built the first LifeLoop prototype — a fully automated incubator that performs every process internally. Candling via computer vision. Automated dud removal. Zero chamber exposure required.

Provincial Finalist

One of Six Teams Selected

LifeLoop was chosen as a provincial finalist — one of only six teams across Ontario selected to present at the Western Fair District Agricultural Showcase.

The Showcase

Real Farmers. Real Demand.

Presenting to actual farmers and AGtech CEOs made the commercial potential undeniable. Multiple industry leaders — including the judges — encouraged us to take LifeLoop beyond a competition project.

March — AGrobotics Finals

🥇 First Place — Ontario

LifeLoop won first place across Ontario. The win confirmed what we were already beginning to understand — this project had genuine startup potential.

Now — Building V3

Taking It to Market

We've been doing serious market validation: emailing farmers, meeting startup investors, and analyzing competitors. We've decided to build LifeLoop V3 — a market-ready product. We're now seeking funding, strategic guidance, and industry connections.
